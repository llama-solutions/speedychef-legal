About SpeedyChef

SpeedyChef is an iOS app that helps you cook with AI-generated recipes based on ingredients you have. You can manage your kitchen storage, save recipes, and subscribe to SpeedyChef Pro for an ad-free experience, unlimited recipe generation, and iCloud sync across your devices.

Key Features

  • AI Recipe Generation: Get recipes based on your available ingredients (free: limited generations per day; Pro: unlimited)
  • Storage: Track and organize your kitchen ingredients
  • Saved Recipes: Save and revisit your favorite recipes
  • SpeedyChef Pro: Ad-free, unlimited recipes, and iCloud sync for ingredients and saved recipes
